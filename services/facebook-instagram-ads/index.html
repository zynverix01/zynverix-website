What we can't promise

We don't guarantee a number of leads, a cost per lead, or a return on ad spend. Ad spend is paid directly to the platform and is separate from our fees. All ads must follow Meta's advertising policies, including the special rules for housing-related ads.

FAQ

Questions about facebook & instagram ads.

Who pays for the ads?

Ad spend is paid directly to Meta from your own ad account. Our fees cover the planning, creation, and management agreed in your proposal.

Do you run Google Ads?

Our lead generation service currently focuses on Facebook and Instagram.

What ad budget do I need?

It depends on your market and goals. We'll recommend a starting budget during the audit. Ad spend is always paid directly to Meta from your own account.

Do your ads follow Meta's rules?

Yes. All ads follow Meta's advertising policies, including the Special Ad Category rules for housing-related ads.

How much does facebook & instagram ads cost?

Pricing depends on your scope and the tools involved. Book a free 15-minute lead-response audit and we'll recommend a setup and give you a clear quote before any work starts.

How long does setup take?

It depends on your lead sources, tools, and how much needs to be built. We'll give you an expected timeline after the audit, before you commit to anything.

Do you only work with real estate and home services?

Those are our focus, but the same approach works for most local businesses that get enquiries online. If you're not sure, book a free audit and ask.
